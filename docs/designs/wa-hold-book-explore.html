The law

Commitment is an overlay. Exploration is never cancelled by it.

1. Overlay, not a lock

A hold or a booking sits on the lead. Phase can still be discover or focused. The bot must not pretend they are a new buyer.

2. Primary chrome = the job

On hold → hold details, papers, file. Booked → payment plan, possession, RM. Never Help me find a home as door 1.

3. Explore is always a door

Third button / last list row: Other projects. Looking around does not cancel the hold. Dropping the hold is its own tap.

4. Papers follow the unit
