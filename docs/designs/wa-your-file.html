Design note (before code)

Layers to touch, and why — not a new “profile FSM”.

wa-pack.tsProbe + brief Flow: copy/CTA = “Set your brief”. After visit_booked: list Your file (visits / brief / add another). Returning greet: same two read-backs when anything is on file.
compose.tsSize probe no longer says “How many bedrooms” when the chrome is the three-slot sheet. visit_recall / recall_constraints already know how to speak — they need evidence, not new copy.
turn.tsAdd a visit is visit_book for a next stop — do not hijack same-project day-ask into empty recall. fetchVisitRecall unions thread visitBookedCache with Desk itinerary so “Done” and “Your visits” cannot disagree.
nayadesk adapterItinerary must read what recordVisit wrote (scheduled_at / confirmed), not only collected.proposed_iso_datetime.
speech-act catalogChip wa.brief.yours → recall_constraints. Open speech (“what do you know about me”, “my requirements”) is a teach/embed kind — not a new regex primary.

Why this layer

  • Bedroom copy vs full brief is a chrome/job mismatch in pack + compose, not understanding.
  • Empty “I don’t see a confirmed visit” is evidence: write/read + hijack, not missing copy.
  • Read-backs already exist: visit_recall, recall_constraints. The buyer has no tap after Confirm, and playground never seeds buyerLifecycle.

Why not other layers

  • Not a new WhatsApp Flow for “your file” — nothing to pick or upload.
  • Not trip logistics / RERA / price inside the brief Flow.
  • Not primary regex for “when is my visit planned?” — that already binds visit_recall; the list was empty.
  • Not compose-only: saying “Your visits: …” without filling the itinerary repeats the lie.
