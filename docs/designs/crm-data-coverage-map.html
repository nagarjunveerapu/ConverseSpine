CRM project-data coverage — what the bot can actually reach

NayaDesk holds ~45 project dimensions across 9 tables. ConverseSpine surfaces ~20. The unreached half is the advisory / investment intelligence — the "advisor, not chatbot" differentiator. Sample questions per row = a ready deep/adversarial test set. · 2026-07-25

The headline: the bot answers the catalog facts (price, RERA, possession, configs, location POIs) well — but it is blind to the market/investment layer the CRM already stores: appreciation %, rental yield bands, corridor maturity, market drivers, expected ROI, revenue/operator model. That layer is dead data — not shipped by Desk's conversationContext and/or not mapped by the bot's adapter. It's why the bot fabricated a rental yield (C1): the real, sourced number exists in micro_market_intel, just unwired. Wiring it (with its confidence + as_of) turns a decline/fabrication into an honest grounded advisory answer.

What this means for testing & quality

  • The adversarial suite tested the covered half (honesty on price/RERA/possession) — and it's now solid. The deep-conversation quality gap is the dead half: a buyer asking real advisory questions (yield, appreciation, ROI, "is this a good investment", "who operates it") gets a deflection or a fabrication, not the truth the CRM holds.
  • Highest-leverage next move: wire micro_market_intel (appreciation + rent bands + drivers, carrying confidence + as_of) and the project investment fields through Desk's conversationContext → the bot's adapter → an honest advisory answer. This is a two-repo change (Desk ships it, CS reads it) and it converts C1's fabrication into a grounded answer.
  • Then the deep/adversarial tests write themselves — each 🔴 row's sample question becomes a test: does the bot now answer from real data (with the source/date), decline honestly when a specific project lacks it, and never fabricate?
