The short answer

FAQs are project-boundverified

Three independent bindings, all present:

And behaviourally: across 39 turns, deliberately including four journeys that ask a project about a concept only a different project has — plantation and coffee yield asked of an apartment tower; operator and revenue model asked of a Bangalore project; towers and club membership asked of a plantation plot — not one reply contained another project's fact.

So what was the Ayana line?

A search-result card, not an FAQ. The annotation comes from NayaDesk's own re-ranker:

The pattern across all five: when the bot cannot answer, its default is to repeat the overview card. That reads as a non-sequitur, and it is indistinguishable from a bug to the buyer.

How I graded this

I read every reply and asked one question: does it contain the fact the buyer asked for? Not "is it on topic" — the overview-card repeats are all perfectly on topic and answer nothing. The only mechanical check in the probe flags which other catalog project names appear in a reply, and every one of those flags turned out to be a shortlist card, which I then read to confirm.

What this run does not covercaveat

Build under test: main + #157, version 919b512d. PR #157 merged at 14:03 and CI redeployed dev at 14:04, over the top of my fix/intel-gated-decline build from 13:47. So the yield line in these transcripts is the old copy — that is expected, not a regression, and the fix is verified separately on the PR.

Single tenant: naya-advisor only. Single channel: /chat. Nothing here says anything about WhatsApp or the SPA.

Not a routing diagnosis. I have shown which phrasings lose the subject, not why. Naming the mechanism means reading the routing path, and I have been wrong three times today by reasoning ahead of the evidence.

What I would fix, in order

  1. The phrasing cliff. A focused project must survive "when is possession". This is the one that costs real conversations, and the contagion makes a single miss terminal.
  2. The 44 orphaned rows. banks / maintenance / payment_process — the bot declines answers it holds, today, on the most common financial question a buyer asks.
  3. The overview-card default. A non-answer dressed as an answer is worse than a decline; the failure-as-value work already has the right shape for this.

On the original question: the binding is sound. I would not spend anything on it.
