The thresholds had to be re-measured live

Vectorize does not return exact cosine — a vector queried against itself scores about 0.87–0.90, not 1.0. So the offline thresholds, correct on paper, collapsed on the live index: at the offline bind bar of 0.920, only 102 of 600 holdout questions (17%) would bind. We ran 600 holdout questions through the deployed worker's real probe path and read the thresholds off the live curve instead:

ThresholdValueLive coverageLive precisionRole
bind0.8984241 of 600 (40%)98.3%the verdict is trusted outright
gap-fill0.8557481 of 600 (80%)95.2%only fills a topic hint when regex found nothing

Live top-1 accuracy is 88.7% — 532 of the 600 answered with the right intent as the first match (offline it was 92 of 100; the lossy index costs a little, most of the gain survives). On the 25 graded real buyer turns, 6 bind at the bind bar and all 6 are correct — zero wrong binds. That is the precision-first trade this model was chosen for.
