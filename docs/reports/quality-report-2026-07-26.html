Open, and deliberately not fixed

The comparator gives class precedence over score spec, not code

classify.ts:190 sorts definition candidates ahead of everything at score ≥0.8, while the comment two lines above says "score still wins". And looksLikeDefinitionAsk fires on a bare "what's", so the boost is on for the commonest phrasing of a factual lookup:

BOOST  what's the price
BOOST  what's the RERA number
BOOST  what's the possession date for Eldorado

I implemented a 0.03 margin and reverted it: failure-routing.test.ts:121 pins a genuine literacy ask with a 0.069 gap that my constant broke. Choosing that constant is a judgement about which utterances are literacy asks — the exact category I got wrong twice today. Parked as a specification with two honest routes: measure the score separation, or state-condition it (LLD Phase 2).
